Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What Creator OS is

Creator OS is personal desktop software used by its author only. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Google Calendar: reads the author's events and can create or update time blocks scheduled in the app.
  • YouTube (read-only): channel details, the list of videos and their statistics, and YouTube Analytics reports.
  • Instagram / Facebook (read-only, when connected): profile details, posts, reels, stories and their insights.

It never uploads, edits or deletes content, and it does not access payments, revenue, messages or contacts.

Where data is stored

All data stays on the author's own Mac. Access tokens are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key held in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is sent to servers run by Creator OS — there are none. Data is exchanged only directly with Google and Meta.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising.

Google API Services

Creator OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Every connection can be removed in the app (Connections → Disconnect), which also revokes the permission at Google or Meta. Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google Account or Facebook settings. Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions: via the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
