Creator OS

Creator OS is a private desktop app (macOS) built by Yam Olisker for personal use as a content creator: tasks, calendar, content planning and analytics for the author's own channels.

It is not offered to the public. It connects only to accounts owned by its author, with read-only access to analytics (and the author's own calendar), and keeps all data on the author's own computer.
